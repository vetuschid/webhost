# AI Consultant Service Types — Scope Reference

Use this file when generating documents to ensure scope, language, and
expectations match the specific service type.

---

## AI Readiness Audit

**What it is:** A structured assessment of a company's current state,
processes, data, tools, and team readiness for AI adoption.

**Typical deliverables:**
- Readiness scorecard across key dimensions
- Gap analysis report
- Prioritized recommendations roadmap
- Executive summary presentation

**Key intake questions to add:**
- Do you have documented processes for your core workflows?
- Where is your data stored, and is it structured or unstructured?
- Are there compliance or data privacy regulations you operate under?
- Who in leadership is sponsoring this AI initiative?

**Typical scope boundaries:**
- Includes: interviews, process review, tool audit, written report
- Excludes: implementation, vendor selection, software licensing

**Timeline:** 2–4 weeks typical

---

## AI Strategy & Roadmap

**What it is:** Co-creation of a 6–18 month AI adoption strategy tailored
to business goals, resources, and risk tolerance.

**Typical deliverables:**
- Strategic AI roadmap document
- Use case prioritization matrix
- Build vs. buy recommendation
- Change management considerations
- ROI projection framework

**Key intake questions to add:**
- What are your top 3 business priorities for the next 12 months?
- What's your annual tech/software budget ballpark?
- Who are the key decision-makers for new technology investments?
- Have you evaluated any AI vendors or platforms already?

**Typical scope boundaries:**
- Includes: workshops, research, roadmap document, presentation
- Excludes: hands-on implementation, ongoing support

**Timeline:** 4–8 weeks typical

---

## AI Tool Implementation

**What it is:** Hands-on setup, configuration, and integration of specific
AI tools into the client's existing workflows or tech stack.

**Typical deliverables:**
- Configured AI tool(s) (e.g., custom GPT, Claude integration, n8n/Make flows)
- Integration documentation
- User guide for the client team
- 1–2 training sessions

**Key intake questions to add:**
- What tools do you want to implement, or are you open to recommendations?
- What does your current tech stack look like? (CRM, project management, comms)
- Who will be the internal owner of this tool after implementation?
- Do you have API access or developer resources available?

**Typical scope boundaries:**
- Includes: setup, config, basic integrations, documentation, handoff
- Excludes: ongoing maintenance, custom software development, vendor costs

**Timeline:** 2–6 weeks depending on complexity

---

## AI Workflow Automation

**What it is:** Identifying and automating repetitive, manual business
processes using AI-powered automation tools (Make, n8n, Zapier + AI, etc.)

**Typical deliverables:**
- Automation map of current vs. future workflows
- Built and tested automation flows
- Testing report
- Handoff documentation and training

**Key intake questions to add:**
- Which tasks does your team repeat most often each week?
- Roughly how many hours per week do you estimate are spent on manual/repetitive work?
- What triggers these tasks? (email, form submission, calendar, manual action?)
- Are you open to tools with monthly subscription costs, or do you prefer one-time?

**Typical scope boundaries:**
- Includes: discovery, build, test, deploy, documentation for agreed workflows
- Excludes: workflows added after project start, ongoing monitoring

**Timeline:** 3–8 weeks depending on number of automations

## AI Team Training / Workshops

**What it is:** Live or recorded training sessions that upskill a team on
AI tools, prompt engineering, responsible AI use, or specific use cases.

**Typical deliverables:**
- Custom workshop curriculum
- Slide deck and workbook materials
- Recorded session(s)
- Post-training resource pack
- Optional: 30-day follow-up Q&A session

**Key intake questions to add:**
- How many people will attend, and what are their roles?
- What's the current AI knowledge level of the team? (Complete beginner / Dabbler / Some experience)
- Will sessions be live, recorded, or hybrid?
- Any specific tools or use cases you want training focused on?
- Do you have a preferred format? (Half-day workshop, weekly 1-hour sessions, etc.)

**Typical scope boundaries:**
- Includes: curriculum design, materials, delivery of agreed sessions
- Excludes: 1:1 coaching, tool setup, licensing costs

**Timeline:** 1–4 weeks prep, delivery as scheduled

## Ongoing AI Retainer / Advisory

**What it is:** Ongoing monthly engagement where the consultant acts as
the client's fractional AI advisor, available for questions, reviews,
and light implementation work.

**Typical deliverables (monthly):**
- Agreed number of advisory hours
- Monthly AI landscape briefing (optional)
- Review of AI outputs, prompts, or workflows as needed
- Priority access via async messaging

**Key intake questions to add:**
- What kinds of questions or tasks would you most likely bring to this retainer?
- How quickly do you typically need responses? (Same day / Within 48h / Weekly)
- Do you want a monthly check-in call included, or fully async?
- Are there specific projects on the horizon I should know about?

**Typical scope boundaries:**
- Includes: async support, advisory hours, agreed deliverables per month
- Excludes: large implementation projects (quoted separately), 24/7 availability

**Timeline:** Ongoing, monthly rolling or minimum 3-month commitment
