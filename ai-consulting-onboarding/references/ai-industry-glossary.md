# AI Industry Glossary — Natural Usage Reference

Use these terms naturally in onboarding documents when relevant.
Don't overload documents with jargon — pick terms the client will recognize
or benefit from learning in context.

---

## Core AI Concepts

**LLM (Large Language Model)** — AI models like GPT-4, Claude, or Gemini
that understand and generate human language. Use when: discussing AI tools
the client may already use or be evaluating.

**Prompt engineering** — The practice of crafting inputs to AI models to
get better, more reliable outputs. Use when: discussing training or
implementation projects.

**AI workflow automation** — Using AI to handle repetitive tasks
automatically, often connected to other tools via APIs or automation
platforms. Use when: scope involves Make, n8n, Zapier + AI.

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** — A technique where AI retrieves
relevant information from a knowledge base before generating a response.
Use when: discussing custom chatbots or internal AI tools.

**Fine-tuning** — Training an AI model on specific data to make it better
at a particular task. Use when: advanced implementation work is in scope.

---

## Business & Strategy Terms

**AI readiness** — How prepared a company is to adopt and benefit from AI,
considering data quality, processes, team skills, and leadership buy-in.

**Use case** — A specific business problem or opportunity where AI can add
value (e.g., "automating customer support responses" is a use case).

**AI stack** — The combination of AI tools, platforms, and integrations
a company uses or plans to use.

**ROI (Return on Investment)** — The measurable return from investing in
AI — could be time saved, revenue generated, or cost reduced.

**Change management** — The process of helping a team adapt to new tools
or ways of working. Critical in AI adoption projects.

**Tech stack** — The full set of software and technology tools a company
uses to run its business.

---

## Automation & Integration Terms

**API (Application Programming Interface)** — A way for different software
systems to communicate. Enables AI tools to connect with CRMs, databases,
websites, etc.

**Make / Integromat** — A popular no-code automation platform for building
workflows that connect apps and trigger AI-powered actions.

**n8n** — An open-source automation platform, often preferred by
tech-savvy users for its flexibility and self-hosting option.

**Webhook** — A trigger that sends data from one app to another when a
specific event happens (e.g., a new form submission triggers an AI workflow).

**CRM** — Customer Relationship Management software (e.g., HubSpot,
Salesforce). Often a key integration point in AI automation projects.

---

## Audit & Assessment Terms

**Gap analysis** — Identifying the difference between where a company is
now and where they want to be with AI.

**Maturity model** — A framework for assessing how advanced a company's
AI capabilities are, typically across several dimensions.

**Data quality** — How clean, complete, and usable a company's data is —
a major factor in AI success.

**Governance** — Policies and processes for how AI is used responsibly
within an organization (privacy, bias, oversight).

**Compliance** — Adhering to regulations relevant to AI use, such as GDPR
in Europe or industry-specific rules in healthcare, finance, etc.
