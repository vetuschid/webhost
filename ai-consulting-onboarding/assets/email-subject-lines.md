# Welcome Email Subject Lines by Service Type

Choose or adapt based on consultant's tone and client context.
Aim for: specific, warm, forward-looking. Avoid: generic, corporate, boring.

---

## AI Readiness Audit
- "Your AI audit starts now — here's what's next"
- "Excited to dig in — a few things before we start"
- "Your AI clarity journey begins today"
- "Let's find out where AI can really move the needle for you"

## AI Strategy & Roadmap
- "Your AI roadmap is about to take shape — next steps inside"
- "From 'AI someday' to 'AI now' — welcome aboard"
- "Big things ahead — let's build your AI strategy together"
- "Your AI future, mapped out — let's get started"

## AI Tool Implementation
- "Time to build — your AI implementation kicks off here"
- "Let's get your AI stack set up — here's what happens next"
- "From zero to AI-powered — welcome to the project"
- "Your tools are about to get a lot smarter — next steps"

## AI Workflow Automation
- "Let's automate the boring stuff — welcome to the project"
- "Your future self will thank you — automation project starts now"
- "Hours back in your week — let's make it happen"
- "Welcome! Your automation journey starts here"

## AI Training / Workshops
- "Your team's AI glow-up starts here — welcome!"
- "Get ready to think differently about AI — next steps"
- "AI training incoming — here's how to prepare"
- "Your team is about to get seriously good at AI"

## Ongoing Retainer / Advisory
- "Your AI co-pilot is officially on board"
- "I'm in your corner — welcome to our retainer"
- "Your questions just got a lot faster to answer — welcome"
- "Think of me as your AI department — let's get started"

- "Welcome! Here's everything you need to know before we begin"
- "So glad we're doing this — next steps inside"
- "It's official — let's make AI work for your business"
