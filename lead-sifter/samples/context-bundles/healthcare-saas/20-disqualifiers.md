# Disqualifiers

If any of the following are true, the lead is **not** qualified:

- Company has fewer than 25 employees (too small to need our product)
- Company has more than 2,000 employees (out of our ICP)
- Industry is non-healthcare (no fintech, edtech, e-commerce, gaming, etc.)
- Title is individual contributor with no operations scope (SDR, AE, CSM, Marketing Manager)
- Title is C-level with no revenue scope (CFO, CTO, COO without RevOps)
- Geography is outside US/Canada
- Lead works for one of our direct competitors (Gong, Clari, Salesloft, Outreach, RevenueHero)
