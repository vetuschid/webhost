# Ideal Customer Profile

## Firmographics

- **Industry:** Healthcare technology, digital health, health-tech SaaS, life-sciences SaaS
- **Size:** 50–500 employees
- **Geography:** US, Canada
- **Funding:** Series A → C preferred (post-product-market-fit, pre-public)

## Buyer personas

- VP / Head / Director of Revenue Operations
- VP / Head / Director of Sales Operations
- VP / Head of GTM Systems
- Chief Revenue Officer (only if no RevOps lead exists)

## Strong positive signals

- Title contains "RevOps", "Revenue Operations", "Sales Ops", "GTM Systems"
- Company is in HealthTech 100, MedCity Pinnacle 50, or Rock Health portfolio
- Job posting history shows recent RevOps hires
