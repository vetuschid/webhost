# Healthcare SaaS — ICP overview

We sell a B2B SaaS revenue-operations platform to mid-market healthcare technology companies.
Our best customers consistently look like this:

- HQ in the United States or Canada
- 50–500 employees
- Sell to hospitals, payers, providers, or pharma
- The lead's title sits in the revenue-operations / sales-operations / GTM-systems function
- The lead has authority to evaluate or buy infrastructure software

This file plus the others in the folder together define a "qualified" lead.
